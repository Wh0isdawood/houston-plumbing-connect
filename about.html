Houston Plumbing Connect is designed to help homeowners describe a plumbing need and find an independent service provider to contact.

What this website does

We operate as a lead-generation and referral website. We collect service enquiries and may share them with independent third-party plumbing providers who may be able to assist.

What this website does not do

We do not directly perform plumbing work, employ technicians, guarantee a provider's availability, or independently verify every provider credential. The provider is responsible for confirming its identity, licensing, insurance, pricing, appointment and work scope.

Our goal

Make it straightforward for homeowners in Houston and nearby communities to explain what they need and request a connection.
