GOOD TO KNOW

Before you book a provider

Is Houston Plumbing Connect a plumbing contractor?

No. This website helps connect consumers with independent plumbing service providers. We do not directly perform plumbing work.

Are service providers licensed and insured?

Provider credentials vary. Always ask the individual provider for current licensing and insurance information and verify it before hiring.

How much will my plumbing service cost?

Pricing depends on the issue, location, parts and work required. Request a written estimate directly from the provider before authorizing work.

Does submitting a request guarantee service?

No. Requests are subject to provider coverage, availability and acceptance. A provider must confirm the appointment directly.
